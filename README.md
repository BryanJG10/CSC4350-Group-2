## Design Coverage

The wireframes cover:

- Account registration, sign-in, and profile management.
- Dining hall, station, and menu browsing.
- Search, filtering, ratings, and reviews.
- Saved favorites and Heads-Up notifications.
- Staff record management, review moderation, and analytics.
- Shared confirmation and recovery states.
- Mobile item details.
- Proposed nutrition, meal recommendation, and food preference features.

## Design Status

These are static wireframes, not a working application. Menu items, ratings, schedules, nutrition values, and review counts are illustrative.

Nutrition and recommendation features are proposed extensions that require agreement on requirements and data-model support. The guide identifies additional decisions to resolve before implementation.

The documents describe intended behavior and suggested validation checks; they do not demonstrate completed implementation or passing tests.
